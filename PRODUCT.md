# AuraShades

**What it is:** a freemium library of live WebGL shader backgrounds (gradient, grain, pastel, aurora, dither, ASCII, grid, light, liquid, texture, orbit, spectral, landscape, wave) for product websites. Browse, tune palette and motion, copy as a React component, raw GLSL, or CSS palette.

**Who it is for:** front-end developers and designers building marketing sites, product launches and portfolios who want living backgrounds without writing shaders.

**Main action:** Browse the library (free), then upgrade to Pro for the full set and React components.

**Plans (assumed, to confirm):** Free (16 shaders, at least one per style, GLSL + CSS copy), Pro $12/mo or $96/yr (all shaders, React components, commercial licence), Team $36/mo (5 seats, shared palettes).

**Surface modes:** Landing and pricing = Persuade. Library = Experience. Editor = Operate. Docs = Read.

**Look:** signal on black: dark-first (near-black #030407 ground, hairline grids, spec-sheet stats), light theme as the alternate. The brand's signature material is Signal Flow, a twisting ribbon of hairline light in electric blue #3d63ff, cyan #38cfff and pink #ff5aa5. Layout, depth and motion rules follow the Nectar design system. Headlines are Schibsted Grotesk italic caps; reading text is Geist, specs are Geist Mono. The navbar is one dark shell that morphs into its panels (after Amey's Morphing Navbar). The mark is a disc whose lower half breaks into scanline bands.
