# AuraShades

**What it is:** a free, open source (MIT) library of live WebGL shader backgrounds (gradient, grain, pastel, aurora, dither, ASCII, grid, light, liquid, texture, orbit, spectral, landscape, wave) for product websites. Browse, tune palette and motion, copy as a React component, raw GLSL, or CSS palette.

**Who it is for:** front-end developers and designers building marketing sites, product launches and portfolios who want living backgrounds without writing shaders.

**Main action:** Browse the library, tune a shader, copy it. Secondary: star or contribute on GitHub.

**Licence:** MIT. Every shader, the React component and all exports are free; no accounts or paid tiers.

**Surface modes:** Landing = Persuade. Library = Experience. Editor = Operate. Docs = Read.

**Look:** a quiet white page with one warm light in it, after dusk.bot, ObsidianUI and Amey's Lumen Lab. White ground, hairline section rules with mono labels, Geist for every word and one Instrument Serif italic phrase per headline. The only colour is the Dune gradient (sunset strata under grain) on the hero stage and the closing band. Black pill buttons mark the one action. The navbar is one dark shell that morphs into its panels, with no underlines. Dark theme is the alternate. Layout, depth and motion rules follow the Nectar design system.
