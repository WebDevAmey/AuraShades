Scope: whole AuraShades site (home, library, shader pages with the editor, pricing, docs, header, footer). Home and pricing are Persuade; library is Experience; editor is Operate; docs is Read.
Audience: front-end developers and designers choosing a live background for a launch page. Action: browse the free shaders, then upgrade to Pro.
Confirmed answers: dark-first redesign following the user's references (middle AuraShades concept, Nexora and techno-company decks); navbar ported from the user's Morphing Navbar; whole site.
Constraints: keep every route, all copy facts, the editor behaviour and the single-WebGL engine; no copying of the reference identities' marks, names or copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Signal on black. AuraShades presents itself as a spec sheet for light: italic caps headlines, hairline grids, big numbers, and one live material, a band of electric colour breaking into pixels. It refuses soft gradient blobs and equal rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #030407, panels #0b0d13 / #10131b, white hairlines at 8%; electric #3d63ff, cyan #38cfff and pink #ff5aa5 carried by the Signal Mosaic shader and the .signal-text gradient. Schibsted Grotesk italic caps for headlines, Geist for text, Geist Mono for specs. Shell and boards 18px, cards 14px, media 8px, controls 10px; 12px gutters; 96 to 160px between sections.

STORY: The visitor sees the mosaic field and the headline, understands that AuraShades sells live shader backgrounds they can tune, believes it because every tile on the page is running, and browses the free set or buys Pro.
