Scope: whole AuraShades site (home, library, shader pages with the editor, pricing, docs, header, footer). Home and pricing are Persuade; library is Experience; editor is Operate; docs is Read.
Audience: front-end developers and designers choosing a live background for a launch page. Action: browse the free shaders, then upgrade to Pro.
Confirmed answers: light gallery ground first (dark stays as the alternate theme); whole site; a new original mark.
Constraints: keep every route, all copy facts, the editor behaviour and the single-WebGL engine; no violet in brand chrome; no copying of the reference identities' marks, names or copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: AuraShades presents itself as its own identity book: every section is a board pinned to a pale gallery wall, and the living grain field is the brand's signature material. It refuses the dark centred headline floating over a shader with a row of equal feature cards under it.

OWN-WORLD: Gallery ground #efefec with boards in #f8f8f6 and hairline rules; ink navy #0c1322; the signature field is hot orange #ff5e3a, cobalt #3443d4 and navy under heavy film grain, always live. One grotesque family for display and text, tabular numerals for values. Boards 20px radius, inner cards 12px, controls 10px; a 4px spacing grid with 16px gutters between boards and 96 to 128px between sections. Solid ink buttons, one orange emphasis per screen.

STORY: The visitor sees a brand board, understands that AuraShades sells live shader backgrounds they can tune, believes it because every tile on the page is running, and browses the free set or buys Pro.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 12-column board grid under a quiet header. Left, an 8-column hero board filled with the live grain field; the new mark, the h1 and two actions set in white at its lower left. Right, two stacked 4-column boards: a palette specimen with four swatches and hex values that re-tint the hero field when picked, and a live library tile linking to the library. Primary action "Browse free shaders" sits on the field.

FORM: brand-identity presentation boards, pinned by the user's reference (position 1 of 1). No concept roll ran. The user pinned the direction in their own words ("use this kind of brand identity for aurashades", with the Lumora identity boards attached), and a user-pinned direction beats the roll, so no seed exists to record. seed: none (user-pinned direction, no roll)
